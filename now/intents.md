# intents — the outcomes we're driving toward

> **Intents** are the *why behind the work* — the outcomes the owner is driving toward. Issues
> **ladder up** to them: an issue sets `intent: <slug>` and the board shows it, so you can
> always see *what a task is for*. Intents may be **business or personal** — this is a whole
> operating layer, not just work.
>
> Durable mission and targets live in [`brain/operating-system.md`](../brain/operating-system.md)
> (captured by grilling — never invented here). This file is the **operational framing**: the
> current intents the weeks + issues serve. Sparse is fine.
>
> Each intent declares a home **area** (`personal` · `business` · `tech`) and a **status**
> (`active` · `completed` · `parked`) on the line under its heading. Don't hand-list issues
> here (that drifts) — the slug is the link; find an intent's work on the
> [board](issues/INDEX.md).
