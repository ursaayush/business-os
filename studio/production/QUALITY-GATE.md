# QUALITY-GATE — the bar every finished piece passes before it ships

> **Read this twice per piece.** Once *before* starting — as the brief, so you aim at the bar
> instead of patching toward it. Once *at the end* — as the audit, writing a short verdict.
> The verdict is a **report; the owner takes the final call** — a too-strict fail gets shown
> to them, not silently ground on.

## The gate (all pieces — video, post, email, page)

1. **Simple?** Every sentence passes [`../voice/WRITING-CONSTANTS.md`](../voice/WRITING-CONSTANTS.md)
   — plain words, no hype, sounds like a person.
2. **On-positioning?** It matches [`../POSITIONING.md`](../POSITIONING.md) — right audience,
   right promise, right tone. (Before positioning is locked: does it match what the owner has
   actually said in `brain/`?)
3. **True?** Every claim is real and backable. Nothing invented, nothing exaggerated.
4. **Complete?** Watched / read start-to-finish in its final form — not assumed from the parts.
5. **Would the owner proudly put their name on it?** If unsure, the answer is no — say what's
   weak and fix it or flag it.

## Extra checks for video

6. **The first 3 seconds earn the next 30.** Something moves, someone talks, or the hook lands
   — no dead air, no slow logo.
7. **Sound is clean.** Levels even, no hiss, music under the voice, never over it.
8. **Cut the fat.** Every second the viewer would skip, you cut first. Shorter is almost
   always better.
9. **Export checked.** The final file itself was played — right format, right size, no glitch.

## The verdict (write it, every time)

```
GATE VERDICT — <piece> — <date>
Pass/flag per check (1–9), one line each where flagged.
Overall: SHIP / FIX FIRST / OWNER'S CALL (+ what the call is)
```

A generalizable lesson learned while gating? Update this file (or the playbook), not just the
draft — that's how the bar gets sharper with every piece.
