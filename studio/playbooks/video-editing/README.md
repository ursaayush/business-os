# Video editing on a Mac — the starter playbook

> For a complete beginner on a MacBook. Read top to bottom once; after that, use it as a
> checklist. The AI can walk you through any step — just ask ("help me edit this video").
> Switching the background with a green screen has its own page:
> [`GREEN-SCREEN.md`](GREEN-SCREEN.md).

## 1. Pick your editor (start free)

| App | Cost | Best for | Verdict for you |
|---|---|---|---|
| **iMovie** | Free (App Store, made by Apple) | Simple cuts, titles, music | **Start here.** Easiest to learn; you can make good videos with it today. |
| **CapCut** | Free (capcut.com) | Social videos — auto-captions, effects, vertical formats | **Add it second.** Best free tool for TikTok/Reels/Shorts style, and the easiest green screen. |
| **DaVinci Resolve** | Free (blackmagicdesign.com) | Professional everything | Later, if you outgrow the others. Pro-grade but a steeper learning curve. |

Don't agonize — start with iMovie today; switching later is easy because the *craft* (below)
is the same in every app.

## 2. Stay organized (do this before anything else)

One folder per video, always the same shape:

```
Videos/
  2026-08-my-first-video/
    footage/     ← the clips from your phone/camera
    audio/       ← music, voiceover
    exports/     ← the finished files
```

Copy footage off your phone before editing. Never edit the only copy of anything.

## 3. The editing workflow (the same 7 steps, every video)

1. **Watch your footage once, fully.** Note the good moments — jot timestamps on paper or
   tell the AI and it'll keep the list.
2. **Rough cut.** Drop the good parts on the timeline in story order. Don't polish yet —
   just get the shape: beginning, middle, end.
3. **Tighten.** Now cut hard: every pause, stumble, and dead second goes. Jump cuts (visible
   little skips between sentences) are completely normal — every YouTuber uses them.
4. **Sound.** Sound matters *more* than picture — viewers forgive soft video, never bad
   audio. Even out volume, remove hiss if you can, add music **quietly under** the voice.
5. **Titles & captions.** A title at the start; captions if it's for social media (most
   people watch with sound off — CapCut auto-generates them).
6. **One color pass.** Auto-enhance in iMovie or CapCut is fine at the start. Consistency
   beats fancy.
7. **Export & check.** Export 1080p. Then **watch the exported file start to finish** before
   sharing — always. This is the quality gate
   ([`../../production/QUALITY-GATE.md`](../../production/QUALITY-GATE.md)).

## 4. Beginner rules that make videos instantly better

- **Hook first.** The first 3 seconds decide if anyone stays. Start with the most interesting
  moment or sentence — never a slow logo or "hey guys, welcome back".
- **Shorter is better.** When unsure, cut. The video you think is "too short" is usually right.
- **Cut on action.** Cutting while something moves hides the cut.
- **Record 10 seconds of room silence** whenever you film — it helps smooth audio edits later.
- **Phone footage is fine.** A modern phone, near a window, at eye level, beats an expensive
  camera badly used. Wipe the lens first.
- **Done beats perfect.** Publish, learn, make the next one. Video 10 will embarrass video 1 —
  that's the plan working.

## 5. Learn by doing (the first-video exercise)

Film yourself for 2 minutes explaining what your business does. Then: rough cut → tighten to
under 60 seconds → music under it → captions → export → watch it back. That one exercise
teaches 80% of everything above. Ask the AI to coach you through it step by step.
