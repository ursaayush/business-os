# Start here 👋

Welcome! This folder is your **business operating system**. Think of it as a very organized
notebook that an AI assistant knows how to run for you. It keeps track of what you're working
on, remembers your decisions, captures your ideas, and helps you do the actual work — starting
with video editing.

You don't need to be technical. The AI does the technical parts and explains everything in
plain words.

## Your first step (the only thing you need to do)

1. Install **Claude Code** (the AI assistant) — ask the person who gave you this folder to
   help, or download it from https://claude.com/claude-code (there's a desktop app for Mac).
2. Open **this folder** in Claude Code.
3. Type: **"set me up"** — and just follow along. The AI will guide you through everything,
   one small step at a time, and it will explain anything you don't understand.

That's it. Everything below this line is managed by the AI — you never have to touch it.

---

## Getting set up (the AI ticks these off with you)

- [ ] **1. Meet & tour** — the AI introduced itself and gave you the 2-minute tour of this folder.
- [ ] **2. Personalized** — your name and your business's name are filled in ([`OWNER.md`](OWNER.md)).
- [ ] **3. Backed up on GitHub** — your folder is safely copied to your own free GitHub account,
      so nothing is ever lost even if the laptop breaks.
- [ ] **4. Automation on** — the two little helpers are installed (they keep the weekly rhythm
      going and tidy the board every morning, automatically).
- [ ] **5. Your business captured** — you did a first "grill me" interview, so the AI knows what
      your business is, who it's for, and what you're aiming at.
- [ ] **6. First week planned** — your first week file has a real focus and your first issues
      are on the board.

When all six are ticked, the system is live. From then on, just open this folder in Claude Code
and talk to it like a colleague: *"what's the plan today?"*, *"help me edit this video"*,
*"save this insight"*, *"I decided X"*.

## What lives where (plain-words map)

| Folder | What it is |
|---|---|
| `brain/` | Your thinking — what your business is, decisions you've made, ideas you've saved. The AI only writes here what **you** actually said. |
| `studio/` | Your methods — how you do things well. Starter guides live here (video editing, green screen). It grows as you work. |
| `now/` | What's happening — this week's focus, the to-do board, what shipped. Always current. |
| `memory/` | The AI's own notes about how you like to work. |
| `scripts/` | The two little automatic helpers. You never touch these. |
