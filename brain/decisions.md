# Decisions — the log

> Every meaningful call gets **one dated line + the why**. Cheap to write now, priceless later
> ("wait, why did we choose X?"). Newest on top. Agents append here whenever the owner makes a
> call in conversation — and never re-litigate a logged decision without new information.

Format: `- **YYYY-MM-DD — <the decision>.** <the why, one line.>`
